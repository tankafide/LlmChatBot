Best practice is: after Send, keep the new user message around the upper third of the reading area and show the beginning of the assistant reply directly beneath it. For a long reply, users should start at its beginning—not its last line.

Your app currently does the opposite: it scrolls to the absolute bottom whenever a reply arrives, so a long completed response lands with only its tail visible above the composer. That explains exactly what you’re seeing.

The behavior I’d implement:

- Keep the composer fixed and reachable—it already is.
- On send, position the user’s message high enough that the pending indicator and reply can appear below.
- When the reply completes, align to the start of the assistant response, not the response’s end.
- If someone has manually scrolled up to read history, never yank them back down. Show a small “Jump to latest” button instead.
- Keep keyboard focus in the message box; scrolling should not steal focus.
- Announce a concise “Reply received” status accessibly, rather than having a screen reader re-read a large response. WAI-ARIA specifically treats chat as a live-update use case and cautions against scrolling focused content away without user action. [WAI-ARIA guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/wai-aria-1.1/)

I documented the exact focused UI change and Playwright checks in [chat-response-reading-position.md](C:/Users/shane/Documents/Apps/LlmChatBot/docs/plans/chat-response-reading-position.md). I have not changed the UI yet.
